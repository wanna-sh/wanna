---
title: Make music
description: Describe a genre, mood and instruments and get an original piece of music.
---

**Music & sound** turns a description into an audio clip.

1. Install a music model from **Get models → Music & sound**. MusicGen models are tested with wanna.sh: *small* is quick,
   *medium* and *large* sound better but take longer.
2. Open **Music & sound** and describe the genre, mood and instruments, for example *"calm lo-fi beat with soft piano
   and vinyl crackle"*.
3. Choose a **Length** (**5**, **10**, **20** or **30 seconds**) and press **Create**.

Longer pieces take longer to make. Finished clips can be played, saved and found again in the **Gallery**.
