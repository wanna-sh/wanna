---
title: Read text aloud
description: Paste text and get a natural sounding recording you can save.
---

**Read aloud** turns text into speech.

1. Install a voice model from **Get models → Read aloud**. MMS-TTS models are small and fast, with one voice per
   language. Bark is larger and more expressive, with several voices.
2. Open **Read aloud**, paste or type your text and press the **Read aloud** button.
3. Play the recording, or save it.

## Options

| Option | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Voice** | For Bark: **Surprise me** (Bark picks a voice), or **English, voice 1** to **voice 4**. Other models have a single voice. |
| **Speed** | **Slower**, **Normal** or **Faster**. |

:::note
wanna.sh only lists voice model types that have been tested end to end, so everything you can download from **Get
models → Read aloud** is known to work.
:::
